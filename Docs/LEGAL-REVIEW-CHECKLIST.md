# Legal documents: what still needs a human

These six documents are live on the site at `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refunds`, `/complaints`,
`/cookies` and `/data-processing`. They were drafted to fit how Study Hub BD actually works,
and every factual claim in them was checked against the code or against the business facts
supplied. They are **not legal advice** and have **not been reviewed by a lawyer**.

Three things to do before relying on them.

## 1. Fill in the blanks

Every unfilled fact is highlighted in amber on the page, so you can find them by reading the
site. Edit them in `content/site-content.json`.

|---|---|---|
| `[TRADE LICENCE NUMBER]` | privacy, terms, data processing, footer | Your Tangail City Corporation trade licence number |
| `[BIN/TIN NUMBER]` | privacy, terms, data processing, footer | Business Identification Number or TIN |

Also unnamed on purpose: **your hosting provider**. The copy says "our hosting provider" and
promises to name it on request. Name it once you have settled where the site lives.

## 2. Confirm these decisions

Defaults were chosen where no instruction existed. Each is defensible, none is the only
answer, and all of them are commercial decisions that are yours and not mine.

| Topic | Proposed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How long student files are kept | 6 years from the date the file closes, counted from the last service delivered | A refusal usually has to be declared and evidenced in later applications for years afterwards, and the same window covers a fee dispute or a complaint about the service. |
| How long health and police documents are kept | 12 months after the visa decision, then destroyed, held separately from the rest of the file | Medical, TB and police clearance papers have no further use once the decision is made, and they are the most sensitive things in the office. |
| The rest of the retention schedule | Enquiries that never become a case: 24 months. Training enrolment and attendance: 3 years from the end of the course. Payment records: 6 years. Original documents: returned to the student within 30 days of the file closing, with no originals kept. | Each period is the shortest one that still lets Study Hub answer a question a student, a tax officer or a university might reasonably ask later. |
| Complaint acknowledgement time | 3 working days from receiving the complaint | Short enough that a worried student knows they have been heard, long enough to survive a holiday or a counsellor being out of the office. |
| Complaint resolution time | A written answer within 15 working days, a progress update every 10 working days if it runs longer, and a final answer within 30 working days in every case | Most complaints need one file review and one conversation, and a hard outer limit stops a case from drifting. |
| Complaint review stage | If the student is not satisfied, a review by the owner or branch manager, answered within 10 working days | A second pair of eyes inside the business is faster and cheaper for the student than going outside, and an outside body will ask whether you offered it. |
| Refund stage structure | Four stages: 1 registration and counselling, 2 application preparation and submission, 3 visa filing, 4 third party costs paid out on the student's behalf. What is refundable is decided by the last stage completed, not by the outcome of the case. | It ties money to work actually done, so neither side has to argue about effort after the fact. |
| Cooling off window | 7 days from signing the service agreement, as long as no work has started and no third party fee has been paid | Gives a family time to talk it over at home without Study Hub losing money it has already spent. |
| Refund request window and payment time | Request in writing within 30 days of the event. Written decision within 15 working days. Payment within 30 days of the decision, to the person who paid. | Keeps the evidence fresh and gives a bank transfer or cheque realistic time to clear. |
| Breach notification to partner universities | Without undue delay and in any case within 24 hours of becoming aware | A university controller has a 72 hour regulatory clock of its own and needs most of that time for its own assessment. |
| Review cycle for all six documents | Reviewed once a year, and whenever the service or the website changes | The privacy and cookie copy is tied to what the website actually does, so it goes stale the moment the site changes. |

## 3. Have a lawyer read them

Specifically:

- **The Bangladesh statute citation.** Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Ordinance was
  gazetted on 6 November 2025 and passed as an Act in April 2026, but published reporting
  disagrees on the exact short title and date, and some sections were deferred. The documents
  refer to it without citing section numbers and say the citation must be confirmed against
  the official gazette. A lawyer should settle the wording.
- **The regulator names.** The supervisory body and the consumer complaints route are
  described generically because the current names and jurisdiction could not be verified.
- **The refund stages.** The structure is drafted; the amounts and the commercial fairness of
  each stage are yours to set.
- **Limitation of liability** in the terms. This is the clause most likely to be tested, and
  the one most worth a lawyer's hour.

## What was deliberately not claimed

- No statement that Study Hub is GDPR compliant or certified. No such certification exists.
- No claim that Standard Contractual Clauses or an IDTA are already signed. The data
  processing statement says you are ready to execute them in the university's preferred form.
- No Data Protection Officer. The role is described as a data contact, which is what it is.
- No admission, visa, scholarship or band score is guaranteed anywhere, because none can be.
